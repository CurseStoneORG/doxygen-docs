Detailed Description

Per-species movement and body profile — the "each species moves and floats like itself" table of the mob AI pass.

Speeds are blocks per tick and read as the era reference: a calm stroll (vanilla wandering uses the mob's own walk speed, cows cover roughly twice the old flat 0.055 pace, rabbits and ocelots dart) and a flee pace used by the panic goal. Body sizes are the era collision boxes, which the AI actuation sweeps against; the eye height is the look-pitch pivot the 0.15.10 client renders the head tilt around.
