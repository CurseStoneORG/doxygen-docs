Detailed Description

The rotation one actuation pass broadcasts: body yaw, head yaw and head pitch.

The 0.15.10 client renders the body from the move packet's yaw and the head from its headYaw, so the two are independent channels — a mob walking one way must keep its body in the movement direction even while its head tracks a player (body and head in one yaw reads as moonwalking: the model faces the player and slides sideways).
