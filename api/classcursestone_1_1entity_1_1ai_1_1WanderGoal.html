Detailed Description

Picks a random nearby point and walks to it, then rests.

Claims the MOVE flag: while wandering, no other goal may drive movement. Facing is left to the actuation step (face the movement direction) unless a LOOK goal overrides it. The stride speed is the species stroll from the context profile, so a cow trots and a chicken scurries with no per-goal tuning.
