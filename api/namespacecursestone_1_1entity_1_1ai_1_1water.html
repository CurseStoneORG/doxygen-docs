Detailed Description

Vanilla-era water buoyancy model, stage-2 shape.

The stage-1 loop (buoyancy every tick plus a paddle whenever the mob moved) integrates to mobs riding ON the water: the paddle never stops pushing up until the head cell clears the surface, so the body skims the waterline and only the soles touch it. The stage-2 model is a hold-band regulator instead: the mob settles where roughly half its body is submerged, gravity pulls it back under the band, the paddle lifts it from below the band, and inside the band the flotation bob rides — a gentle zero-mean force that rocks the body up and down over the surface instead of freezing the waterline.

◆ kBobTwoPi

constexpr float cursestone::entity::ai::water::kBobTwoPi = 6.2831853f
constexpr

Flotation bob: the gentle, continuous rocking of a body that floats half in and half out of the water.

Inside the hold band the regulator applies this instead of no force at all: a zero-mean vertical force shaped like a slow sine (the body rises and falls over the surface) plus a matching pitch component the move packet renders as the rocking model. Excursions stay regulator-owned — anything beyond the band is corrected exactly as before, so equilibrium, sink recovery and the shore hop behave identically; the bob only replaces the frozen waterline inside the band.

◆ kSubmergeBand

Half-width of the hold band in submersion fraction.

Inside [target-band, target+band] no vertical force applies, so an idle mob floats still instead of oscillating forever; a mob outside the band accelerates back toward it. The band sizes the gentle surface bob while swimming.
